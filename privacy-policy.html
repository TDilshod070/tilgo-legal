Privacy Policy

TilGo Marketing Agent · Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TilGo Marketing Agent application ("the App", "we") collects, uses and protects information when it manages the Instagram professional account @tilgo.marketing on behalf of its owner (TilGo). The App is an internal marketing tool used only by the account owner. It is not offered to the public and does not have end-user accounts.

1. What the App does

The App helps the owner of the Instagram professional account to read and answer Direct messages and comments sent to that account, publish content, and view account statistics. Every outgoing message, comment reply, publication or deletion is reviewed and explicitly approved by a human (the account owner) before it is sent.

2. Information we process

  • Instagram Direct messages sent to @tilgo.marketing: message text, time, sender's Instagram-scoped ID and username.
  • Comments on @tilgo.marketing posts: comment text, time, commenter's username and comment ID.
  • Content and statistics of @tilgo.marketing: posts, captions, likes, comments count, reach and other insights provided by the Instagram Graph API.
  • Public profile information of business accounts retrieved through Business Discovery for competitor analysis (public posts and counts only).

We do not request or collect passwords, payment information, phone contacts or location data.

3. How we use information

  • To show incoming messages and comments to the account owner and prepare suggested replies.
  • To send replies only after the owner approves them.
  • To analyse content performance and prepare marketing reports for the owner.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties for advertising. We do not use the data to build profiles of individuals.

4. Service providers

To draft suggested replies and analyses, message and comment text may be processed by an AI service (Anthropic Claude) acting as a data processor. The data is transmitted over encrypted connections and is used only to generate the requested response.

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored on the account owner's computer. Conversation and comment data is kept only as long as needed to answer the customer and for up to 90 days for context, after which it is deleted. Access tokens are stored locally and are never shared.

6. Your rights and data deletion

If you have messaged or commented on @tilgo.marketing and want your data deleted, send a Direct message with the word "DELETE" to @tilgo.marketing or email us at the address below. We will delete all stored messages and comments associated with your account within 30 days and confirm the deletion. See the Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest version.
