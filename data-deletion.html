TilGo Marketing Agent stores Instagram Direct messages and comments sent to @tilgo.marketing only to answer them. To request deletion of your data:

  1. Send a Direct message with the word DELETE to @tilgo.marketing on Instagram, or
  2. Email dilshodbek2814@gmail.com with your Instagram username and the subject "Data deletion request".

We will delete all messages, comments and related records associated with your Instagram account within 30 days and confirm by the same channel you used.

You can also remove the App's access to your data at any time from Instagram: Settings → Security → Apps and websites.
